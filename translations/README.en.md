I have worked in software since 2017. My experience combines technical leadership, development with C# and .NET, and enterprise systems integration.

I have worked with ERP systems, e-commerce and multi-tenant SaaS platforms. My focus is designing, delivering and evolving software while considering the operations that depend on it.

### Experience and focus

- **Design and development:** enterprise applications with C# and .NET, clearly defined responsibilities and dependencies between components.
- **Integration:** connecting applications and business processes, defining information contracts and planning for failure recovery.
- **System evolution:** incremental change, maintainability and operational continuity.

My previous experience in accounting and operational finance adds a perspective on controls, information integrity and the consequences of change.
